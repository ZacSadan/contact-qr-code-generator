📇 Contact QR Code Generator

Fill in your details once. Anyone can scan the code with their phone camera and save you to their contacts in one tap.

🔒

Your data never leaves your browser.

No server. No database. No uploads. No tracking. This page is plain JavaScript running on your own device. The QR code, the photo and every field you type are created right here, and nothing is sent to us or to any cloud server. That's the whole point, and it's why this is so cool.

Your progress is kept as a draft in this browser only (clear it any time with “Clear form”). If you choose to connect Google, your browser reads your basic profile directly from Google. It never passes through anyone else.

Why is this cool?

🙋

For you

Your contact card, always one tap away. Download the QR code and save it to Google Photos.

Pro tip: use Share to Google Photos. The image is dated 19/09/2037, and because Google Photos sorts by date, your QR code stays pinned at the very top of your gallery.

Want it even closer? Photo-widget apps such as Photo Widget can put it right on your phone's home screen.

📱

For the person scanning

No app, no typing, no spelling mistakes. They just point their phone camera at the code.

The contacts app opens automatically with all your details already filled in: name, phones, emails, websites, company and address. All they do is tap Save.

🔒

For your privacy

Everything happens in your browser. There is no server behind this page, so there is nothing to hack, sell or leak. Your details live only on your device and inside the QR code you choose to share.
